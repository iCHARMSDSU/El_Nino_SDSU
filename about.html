The lab

The El Niño Resource Library is maintained by the SDSU Climate Informatics Lab at San Diego State University, led by Distinguished Professor Sam Shen. The lab develops open, reproducible tools for visualizing and analyzing climate data, including iCHARM, an interactive platform for exploring weather and climate data.

Methods and data

Everything on this site is built from public NOAA data. Anomalies are measured against the 1991–2020 average unless a figure says otherwise.

  • Relative Oceanic Niño Index (RONI), NOAA Climate Prediction Center: official ENSO strength and outlooks
  • ERSST v5 (Extended Reconstructed Sea Surface Temperature), NOAA NCEI: monthly sea surface temperatures back to the 1800s
  • OISST v2.1 (Optimum Interpolation Sea Surface Temperature), NOAA NCEI: daily, higher-resolution sea surface temperatures
  • NCEP GODAS (Global Ocean Data Assimilation System): ocean temperatures below the surface

Each figure's page lists the exact data it uses. Limitations: forecasts are probabilities, not certainties, and records based on data since 1950 may not reflect older events.
